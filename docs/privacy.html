Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

What we collect

Only what you type into the planner form: event type, event name, city, date, guest count, budget, theme and preferences. We do not ask for your name, phone, email, location, payment details or any account.

How it is used

Your inputs are used only to build your plan during your session. To create personalised ideas, the event details (not your identity) are sent to Google's Gemini API. Google processes this under the Gemini API Terms. Budgets, timelines and checklists are calculated inside the app.

Storage

We keep no database. A plan may stay in the server's short-term memory cache for up to one hour to avoid repeat AI calls, then it is discarded. Downloads (PDF, Markdown, calendar) are generated on demand and saved only on your device.

Cookies & tracking

Utsav AI sets no advertising or analytics cookies. The hosting platform (Streamlit Community Cloud) may use strictly necessary session cookies to keep the app running; see its privacy policy. The Microsoft Store app shell stores only an offline copy of its own pages on your device.

Third-party services

  • Streamlit Community Cloud — app hosting
  • Google Gemini API — idea generation
  • GitHub Pages — this website and the Store app shell

Your rights

Because we store no personal data, there is nothing to export or delete. This policy is written to follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the GDPR principles of data minimisation. Questions: kanakprabhakar72@gmail.com.
